Internship Project #1

Flight Simulator Controller Holders

Load Tested

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to design and create a custom holder for flight simulator controllers from scratch. It needed to be compact, sturdy, easily accessible, scalable for a full production run, and visually clean to match the lab environment.

My Role & Responsibilities

I served as the Lead Designer and Production Lead for this workflow. My responsibilities included engineering the initial 3D CAD model entirely from scratch, running mechanical load stress considerations, and overseeing the 3D printing manufacturing setup for all 32 finished units required for the lab deployment.

Challenges & How I Overcame Them

The first challenge was dialing in the tight hardware tolerances. I resolved this by utilizing rapid prototyping and short test prints to systematically dial in the exact physical dimensions through iterative adjustments.

Because the flight controllers are notably heavy, standard bracket mounts faced a high risk of snapping along the layer lines. I overcame this by modifying the print parameters and changing the build orientation of the 3D model. By orienting the layers so that they ran against the directional tension of the heavy load, I maximized the overall shear strength.

Measurable Results & Impact

The measurable result was the successful production run and deployment of all 32 structural mounts. Stress analysis verified each unit can successfully handle continuous vertical loads of up to 10 lbs without physical fatigue. This directly optimized the workspace layout and protected expensive equipment from accidental drops.
