Internship Project #3

Gym Scooter Coupling Bracket

Custom Mechanism

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to design and manufacture a custom clip bracket from scratch to securely join two independent Sports Scooter Boards together. The bracket transformed single scooters into a unified platform for physical education activities.

My Role & Responsibilities

I served as the Lead Designer and Fabricator. My core responsibilities included harvesting structural dimension maps from the physical scooter frames, engineering a parametric CAD model featuring an interlocking snap-fit mechanism, and 3D printing functional iterations to verify seamless fitment.

Challenges & How I Overcame Them

The chief engineering obstacle was creating a latch/hinge system capable of absorbing harsh lateral force shifts and continuous floor vibrations under load. I solved this by adding reinforced structural hinge walls and thick locking lips to safely handle the stress distributions.

Additionally, I had to thoroughly test the elasticity and tolerance constraints of the printed plastic. The component needed to remain completely locked during dynamic student operations while allowing instructors to easily clip the brackets on and off when modifying activities.

Measurable Results & Impact

The final result was a rugged, high-strength coupling mount that linked the boards with absolute zero slippage under full weight conditions. This significantly enhanced the versatility of the existing school equipment assets, allowing safe, creative team sports variations.
