Internship Project #2

3D Printer Fleet Restoration

100% Operational

Project Overview

This project involved the complete mechanical diagnostics, repair, and restoration of all 9 Ender 3 V3 KE 3D printers in the school's manufacturing lab. The objective was to bring the entire down fleet back to full operational status and establish an asset procurement database for spare components.

My Role & Responsibilities

I was placed completely in charge of the 3D printer fleet restoration. My responsibilities included conducting thorough physical hardware audits on each assembly, isolating diverse mechanical and electrical failures, replacing hot-end components, and compiling a comprehensive parts sourcing catalog to ensure long-term maintenance.

Challenges & How I Overcame Them

The primary logistical challenge was tracking nine different sets of hardware failures concurrently without losing layout efficiency. I overcame this by designing a systematic diagnostics checklist for each machine to immediately map out and cross-reference common failures like heat-creep clogs, frame alignment errors, or baseline firmware snags.

Additionally, accurately identifying specific proprietary components for the newer V3 KE model variants proved difficult. I resolved this by parsing through original manufacturer technical schematics to cleanly document precise part numbers, preventing downstream compatibility errors.

Measurable Results & Impact

The measurable results were the successful restoration of all nine lab printers back to pristine manufacturing specifications and the creation of a 100% accurate parts inventory ledger. This directly multiplied the lab's printing capabilities, eliminated project backlogs, and preserved safety standards.
